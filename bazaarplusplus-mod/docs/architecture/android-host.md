# Android Host

`BazaarPlusPlus.Android` (`net10.0`) is the Android Host (root `CONTEXT.md`, Host). It is a MelonMod (`src/BazaarPlusPlus.Android/Host/AndroidMod.cs`) compiled against the Interop Snapshot and the Loader Release (`build/InteropPath.props`), never against `Managed/`.

## Shape

- `Host/` owns the loader entry, the `BppLog` exit over MelonLogger (`MelonLogSink`, scope `Android`), the settings store over `MelonLoader/UserData/BazaarPlusPlus.cfg` (`IniSettingsStore`) and the Data Root (`AndroidPaths`): `BazaarPlusPlusV6` beside the MelonLoader base directory.
- `AndroidComposition` builds Core's `CoreComposition` over that Data Root and registers the record chain and the Bundle path, in start order: Core's `RunLifecycleModule` and `RunLoggingModule` over the IL2CPP probes in `GameInterop/`, then Core's `BundleSealCoordinator`. Core's `UploadPump` starts after them and is ticked from `AndroidMod.OnUpdate`; it sends each sealed Bundle to `POST /bundles` with the `android-aarch64` User-Agent token. Bundles are Run-only: the host takes no end-of-run screenshot.
- `AndroidPvpBattleCapture` frames each PvP battle from the net messages (opening `PVPCombat` game sim, combat sim, next game sim), as the desktop's `CombatReplayCaptureService` does. At the opening it takes the four board captures (`AndroidPvpBattleSnapshots`, in Core's `PvpBattleCardSetDocument` shape), and retries an empty or failed player set live at the combat sim and the close under Core's `PvpBattleCaptureRule`, the desktop's rule; it serializes each message as it arrives (`AndroidReplayMessages`), and saves the Replay Payload to `CombatReplays/`, then the battle through Storage's `PvpBattleSqliteStore`, under the persistence lease of Core's `ReplayPayloadOperationGate`. The main thread never waits on that gate: when the lease stays held past `AndroidPvpBattleCapture.LeaseWait` (a replay holds it for its whole session), the battle is saved with its payload `missing` and `android.pvp_battle.lease_busy` logs it. `android.pvp_battle.recorded` logs each set's label and count and how long the saves held the main thread.
- On every start Core's `ReplayPayloadMaintenanceService` sweeps `CombatReplays/` once on a pool thread, as on the desktop: orphan files, lost files, and the retention of `ReplayPayloadRetentionPolicy`.
- The Bundle composer is Core's `RunPayloadComposer`; the host supplies only `AndroidPvpBattleEvidence` (card enum values, Replay Payload check).
- `Patches/` install per class under the `[Android] PatchMode` setting (`enabled`, `observe`, `disabled`). A class that fails to install degrades only itself. `NetMessageReceivePatch` is the message intake (run initialization, battle capture); `CombatSimulatePatch` publishes `CombatSimObserved`.
- In the `enabled` mode the host adds the interaction features, which touch replaces the desktop's hotkeys for. `Ui/AndroidTouchUi` is a BPP button on the left edge that opens one panel: Settings switches `[Android] UpgradePreview`, `[EnchantPreview] Mode` (`Always` or `Off`; the desktop-only `AutoOnPedestalChoice` reads as `Off`, since the host has no pedestal probe) `[Android] CollectionPanel` and `[Android] LiveBuildPanel`, and shows the game-update row; History closes it and opens the History panel, and the button hides while History is open. `[Android] TouchMenu` hides the button. The panel and its controls are BPP's own uGUI (`Ui/AndroidUiKit`), not the game's menus.
- `GameInterop/CardRental/AndroidCardRental` is the host's one rental of the game's card UI prefabs, as the desktop's native card preview: a panel rents a card (template, tier, enchantment) under one of its RectTransforms, re-targets it, and returns it; `CardRentalCore` keeps the ledger and the per-kind pool, `AndroidCardRentalBackend` instantiates through the game's `AssetLoader` and runs `CardPreviewBase.SetUp`. The melon ticks it every frame and `AndroidMod.OnSceneWasLoaded` releases everything after the touch UI closed its panels; the periodic summary and `android.card_rental.released` carry the counts, and `card_balanced=true` means every rental came back.
- `Collection/AndroidCollectionPanel` is the desktop Collection panel on touch ([collection.md](collection.md)), opened from the menu's Card Collection tab while `[Android] CollectionPanel` is on: Core's `CollectionViewState` drives the same tabs, search, filters, sources, Day chip and hero preference, `AndroidCollectionGrid` lays out Core's `CollectionGridLayout` and rents a card per visible cell, and `AndroidCollectionInspection` re-targets the tapped card to the chosen tier and enchantment. `AndroidCollectionCatalog` builds the catalog once per game language and keeps it for the session. Switching the setting off closes the panel and destroys the pooled cards.
- `LiveBuild/AndroidLiveBuildPanel` is the desktop's Ten-Win Builds panel (Live Build, [panels.md](panels.md)) on touch, opened from the menu's Ten-Win Builds tab while `[Android] LiveBuildPanel` is on. It owns Core's `LiveBuildPanelState` for one open and reads the live boards through `GameInterop/AndroidLiveCardSnapshotReader`; `LiveBuild/AndroidLiveBuildView` renders the snapshot as a full sheet: the pinned recommendation with its pager, then the shop, stash and current board in a drag-scrolled list, and the corpus, hero, metric cells and guidance in a sidebar. `LiveBuild/AndroidLiveBuildBoard` is the native board mapper: each planned card rents the game's prefab through `AndroidCardRental` at its socket, and a re-render re-rents only the cards that changed. A tap replaces the desktop's hover: on a shop, stash or board card it toggles a candidate, on a recommended card it opens `LiveBuild/AndroidLiveBuildInspection`, the card enlarged with its name and authored text. The composition root owns the ten-win Remote Embedded Catalog (`LiveBuild/AndroidTenWinBuildCatalog`, the desktop's parser, URL and cache file name, the seed linked into this assembly) and its `BuildRecommendationRepository`, and disposes it on stop. Supporters are not sampled on Android.
- Touch fit: Core's `Core/Ui/TouchMetrics` turns the screen size, `Screen.dpi` (400 when the device reports none) and `Screen.safeArea` into overlay-canvas units, with 48 dp targets and 12 sp text as floors, and `LiveBuildTouchLayout` lays the Live Build sheet out from it inside the safe area; both are Unity-free and tested in `tests/LiveBuildPanel.Tests`. `Ui/AndroidTouchScreen` reads the metrics and places a rect; the panel re-lays itself out when the screen key (size, dpi, safe area) changes. The back key closes the top-most BPP surface first: `Ui/AndroidBackKey` asks its handlers in order (Live Build, its inspection first; Collection; the touch menu), and `Patches/BackKeyPatches` consumes the game's escape for it, so the game's escape menu opens only when none of them is open. History takes no back press yet.
- `Ui/AndroidHistoryPanel` is the desktop's History panel ported ([panels.md](panels.md)): it owns Core's `HistoryPanelState` and `HistoryPanelCoordinator` for the host's lifetime and renders Core's `HistoryPanelViewModelBuilder` output through `Ui/AndroidHistoryPanelView`, an owned uGUI canvas laid out from Core's `HistoryPanelLayout` with the Runs and Ghost sections, the battle timeline, the pagers, the page label, the board rects with their messages, the status line and the replay action. It stays open across scenes except into a combat, closes when a replay starts, and reopens with its selection once the replay session is over and the lobby is active. The desktop's native art renders flat (hero, rank and trophy as text); the two BPP badge atlases are the desktop's PNGs linked into the assembly. `AndroidComposition.CreateHistoryDependencies` composes it: the repository on the history database, Ghost sync over History's own `ModApiSession`, which the composition owns and disposes last (a session that cannot be built fails the composition's start, ADR-0006), and Core's `HistoryPanelReplayService` over the replay runtime and `AndroidNoRecording`. Favorites, delete, the filters, the More dialog with the account link, and the native boards are issue #399; their rects are reserved and `Ui/IHistoryBoardSurface` is the board seam.
- `CombatReplay/AndroidCombatReplayRuntime` plays a saved or Ghost battle from History: the playback half of the desktop's `CombatReplayRuntime` over Core's `SavedReplayLifecycle`, `ReplayPlaybackPublisher` and `ReplayPlaybackLogOperation`. A saved battle holds the payload gate's playback lease for its session. `CombatReplay/Bootstrap/AndroidReplayBootstrap` bootstraps the game scene from the lobby, injects the battle (the spawn through `NetMessageProcessor.Handle`, the captured boards onto the card models, the opening events), pushes `ReplayState` and calls `Replay()`, logging each stage as `android.replay.bootstrap_stage`. `Events.StateChanged` delivers the state exit; the only exits are the native recap continue and the `ReplayState.Exit` prefix, which returns to the lobby (ADR-0002). `Patches/AndroidReplayPatches` hold the replay's `Task` seams on IL2CPP (`StartRunAppState.FinalizeRunInitialization` returns the game's completed task; the item setup and board spawn tasks are recorded, never replaced) and its visual seams, each counting its hits for `android.hooks.summary`. `AndroidReplayGuard` keeps any message the intake sees during a replay out of the PvP capture and counts it; run lifecycle is untouched. Not ported yet: the desktop's presentation, audio, VFX and soundtrack warmers and its socket-effect restoration.
- `Host/AndroidMainThread` is the main thread as a `SynchronizationContext`, ticked first in `AndroidMod.OnUpdate`. History's and the replay's entry points run inside its scope (every History control through `Ui/AndroidUiActions.OnTap`), so their awaits resume on the main thread; the record chain's pool work keeps its own threads. Game tasks are awaited only through `GameInterop/Il2CppTasks`, which polls them each frame, and History's host ports (`Host/AndroidHistoryPorts`: the clock, the profile account, the `PlayerPrefs` link hint) answer 0 or null off the main thread.
- `GameInterop/AndroidUpgradePreview` puts the item whose tooltip a tap opens into the game's own upgrade preview (`CardController.EnterUpgradePreview`), which renders its next-tier values; it holds at most one controller and releases it when another card opens, the tooltip closes, the switch turns off, or a scene loads. Each render of the held item's tooltip leases a `GameInterop/AndroidUpgradePreviewValueProjection` (the desktop's value projection, aura multipliers included, over Core's `UpgradePreviewValueMath`), keyed by the tooltip data's pointer and returned when the render ends; `AndroidUpgradePreviewValues` renders that tooltip's text with BPP's next-tier values, which the game leaves out for literal ability and aura values, and sets its cooldown clock. The periodic summary's `upgrade_values_leased` and `upgrade_values_returned` match whenever no tooltip is rendering. `GameInterop/AndroidEnchantPreview` renders each enchantment's text against the game's lock-mode preview card (`CardExtensions.ApplyPreviewEnchantment`) and appends it to that render's passive block.
- `Lobby/AndroidVersionCheck` runs on every `MainMenuScene` load: Core's `MainMenuSelfUpdate` over the `android-aarch64` Platform Release Manifest ([self-update.md](self-update.md)), the status on the version label, and, when the build number in the manifest's `game.gameVersion` is above the one in the running game's `Application.version`, the game-update row that opens the APK's URL.
- Shared code comes from Core's `net10.0` output, with Storage and ModApi through it; the host links no Core or desktop source. `just mod::android-deploy` pushes the build to the `melonloader` paths of the Payload Inventory (root `CONTEXT.md`), read through the generated `Payload.targets`: BPP's assemblies into `MelonLoader/Mods/`, third-party ones into `MelonLoader/UserLibs/`, the Updater into `MelonLoader/Plugins/` ([self-update.md](self-update.md)).

Not on Android yet: the favorites, delete, filters, More dialog, native boards and native artwork of the desktop History panel; replay video recording; Live Build's supporter list; and the ability values in an inspected card's text, which Live Build and Collection both drop where the desktop's native tooltip shows them.

## Build and test

`tests/AndroidHost.Tests` fails if the compiled host references BepInEx or a desktop assembly, or defines a BPP type outside `BazaarPlusPlus.Android` (a linked source). It also loads the compiled host and runs the code that never calls into IL2CPP (ADR-0016): History's composition over the DELETE-mode database with a stub Mod API session serving the server's Ghost summary contract, the main-thread pump, the capture's non-blocking persistence, the replay guard, the template index, the presentation task tracker, the threading rules over the host's IL, the Bundle evidence over the interop enums, the composer over the DELETE-mode database, the replay sweep, and the card rental's ledger over a fake backend (`CardRentalCoreTests`). `tests/LiveBuildPanel.Tests` holds the touch layout to its floors at several screen sizes and cutouts (`LiveBuildTouchLayoutTests`). It runs in `just mod::test-core`, the suites that need no Managed snapshot, and in the `mod-android` CI lane, with the two goldens on the `net10.0` binding this host ships: `tests/BundleV6Codec.Net10.Tests` (the Run segment) and `tests/RunLoggingPipeline.Net10.Tests` (the history database), each of which must reproduce the desktop binding's goldens byte for byte. `just mod::android-*` drives one device, and `just mod::android-setting <section> <key> <value>` switches one feature for an on/off capture; `just mod::android-smoke <mode>` asserts injection, hook hit, and feature on it before a release; no CI lane runs a device ([development guide](../../../docs/development.md#android-冒烟)).

## Gotchas

- SQLite's native `libe_sqlite3.so` must sit in the APK's `lib/arm64-v8a/` (the Android release's injection step adds it). External storage is `noexec`, so `UserLibs/` cannot carry it; a deploy to an APK without it fails at the first store open.
- A `T?` an IL2CPP getter returns arrives as an `Il2CppSystem.Nullable` proxy over a boxed `T`, whose `HasValue` and `Value` are wrong: every player attribute read as 0 and opponent ranks came out shifted. Read the dictionary or field behind it, or go through `AndroidGameIds.Unboxed`; the board captures read every card's section, socket, tier, size, and enchantment that way. The other direction throws: a generated setter or constructor unboxes its `Il2CppSystem.Nullable` argument through `Il2CppObjectBaseToPtrNotNull`, so passing `null` for an absent value raised a `NullReferenceException` and the replay lost its opponent portrait. Pass `AndroidGameIds.Optional`, an empty proxy for an absent value.
- A `bool` read through an IL2CPP struct proxy, such as a `ValueTuple`'s `Item2`, reads as true in C#, yet passed back into a game method the game took it as false where a literal `true` worked: the upgrade preview handed the typography's `renderedWithIcon` to `TooltipTypography.ColorKeywords` and drew a second keyword icon after every value (`Damage` after `25»45`). Pass such a bool through `AndroidGameIds.Canonical` first.
- Every Android store opens the database with `AndroidComposition.DatabaseOptions` (`DELETE`): a Storage store given no options would switch the file to WAL. Core's seal coordinator, upload pump, and composer therefore take the options as a required argument. `DELETE` keeps one self-contained file that `adb pull` reads whole. WAL opened, wrote, and read back on the emulator's FUSE Data Root, but that is one process on one device, not a reason to switch.
- A message here is an IL2CPP proxy, which the managed MessagePack cannot read: Replay Payload bytes come from the game's own serializer (`Il2CppMessagePack.MessagePackSerializer.Serialize<T>` with the concrete message type and `MessagePackConfig.Options`, as the desktop's `PvpReplayPayloadFactory`), and each is decoded back before the payload is kept. That runs on the main thread only. The seal worker is a pool thread that must not call into IL2CPP, so `AndroidPvpBattleEvidence` checks a payload for well-formed MessagePack and resolves card enums from the interop enums, which are managed.
- Harmony can replace the IL2CPP `CombatSimHandler.Simulate` `Task`, as the desktop's `CombatSimPatch` does for replay: a prefix that returned an `Il2CppSystem.Threading.Tasks.TaskCompletionSource<Object>`'s `Task` held the combat until BPP completed it from `OnUpdate` after running the original 3 s later, and the game awaited it and finished the fight (#360 spike, removed after the measurement).
- LemonLoader installs no managed `SynchronizationContext`: an `await` started from a melon callback resumes on a pool thread, where a call into IL2CPP is a native fault, not an exception. History's coordinator and the replay runtime publish on the caller's context, so they run under `AndroidMainThread`. Awaiting an `Il2CppSystem` task directly compiles (its awaiter implements `INotifyCompletion`) but resumes outside the pump; `AndroidHostThreadingRuleTests` holds the host's IL to `Il2CppTasks`.
- The online Android build has no `SocketBehavior`: the replay's network host is `Il2CppNetworking.NetworkManager`. The game's private fields the desktop reaches by reflection surface as interop properties (`AppState._gameSimHandler`, `RunManager._baseBoardReference`, `GameMessageHandler`1._handledMessages`).
- Ghost sync counts a downloaded snapshot's templates on a pool thread, where the static data manager must not be called. It reads `GameInterop/AndroidCardTemplateIndex`, which answers true until it is built rather than build itself off the thread. When History opens, the main thread reads only the manager's `_dbPath` and a pool thread reads the ids from that game-data file's `cards` table; the manager's own `GetCardMap` deserializes every card and froze the main thread for 26 s on the emulator.
- `new Il2CppStringArray(0)` and `new Il2CppStructArray<Vector3>(4)` bind the int literal to the interop array's `(IntPtr pointer)` constructor, since `int` converts implicitly to `nint`, and wrap address 0 or 4: History's `GetWorldCorners` buffer crashed the game with a SIGSEGV and the replay's empty opponent loadout lost its portrait. Size an interop array with a `long` (`0L`, `4L`); `AndroidHostInteropRuleTests` holds the host's IL to that.
- A reader of the DELETE-mode database cannot overlap a write, so History's repository opens it with `busy_timeout` and a page read during a write burst waits instead of failing.
- History keeps the desktop's layout on a phone, where a canvas unit is about 1.27 px at 2400x1080: touch targets reach `HistoryPanelLayout.TouchTargetUnits` (about 61 px, short of 48 dp), and the grown record and replay buttons overlap the status line by about 3 units.
- An unreadable `BazaarPlusPlus.cfg` still installs the hooks (`PatchMode` falls back to `enabled`) but starts no record chain: `android.record_chain.start_failed` follows `android.settings.load_failed`, and nothing is recorded, sealed, or uploaded until the file reads.
- The startup `android.build_channel.probe` logs `server_option=missing` on every online build, the Online answer: as on desktop, only the PTR fork of `Config` declares `ServerOption`. It does not show whether Il2CppInterop exposes the nested type; only a PTR or staging Android capture can.
- A managed `MonoBehaviour` exists in IL2CPP only after `ClassInjector.RegisterTypeInIl2Cpp<T>()`, and the type needs the `(IntPtr)` constructor; `AddComponent<T>` on an unregistered type fails. Unity calls the injected `Update`. Its `StartCoroutine` takes an `Il2CppSystem.Collections.IEnumerator`, not a managed iterator: run a managed coroutine through `MelonCoroutines.Start`, where both `yield return null` and `WaitForSeconds` resume.
- `TooltipParentComponent.HideCardTooltipController` returns early while a card-to-card transition runs, and tapping a second card calls it during that transition. Releasing the upgrade preview on every call left the new card's tooltip showing next-tier values with its controller out of preview, so `AndroidUpgradePreview` releases only when the tooltip's `CurrentCard` was cleared.
- Every `SceneLoader.LoadScene` hides the card tooltip before it loads anything (`LoadingStarted` raises `LoadAddressableSceneStarted`, which `TooltipParentComponent` answers with `HideCardTooltipController`), so a held upgrade preview normally ends as `tooltip_hidden`, and pausing the app ends it the same way. Only a load that starts during the 0.25 s card-to-card transition, which also runs when the first tooltip opens, keeps the hold past that early-returning hide; `AndroidMod.OnSceneWasLoaded` then releases it as `scene_changed`, with the controller still alive because the new scene loads before the old one unloads. A probe that calls the game's own `SceneLoader.LoadScene` inside that window exercised this on the device (#402); no player-reachable path was observed, but keep that release defensively, or the controller stays in preview and `upgrade_preview_entered` runs ahead of `exited`.
- A rented card belongs to the asset scope it was instantiated in, and a lobby ↔ run scene change releases the `Current` scope's assets, whitening every instance of it, live or pooled; a re-`SetUp` does not bring the art back. The backend instantiates in `AssetScope.Global` (a real `Il2CppSystem.Nullable<AssetScope>`; the generated method rejects null) and the rental still destroys its pool on every scene load. The instantiate and set-up tasks are IL2CPP `Task`s polled from the frame tick, never awaited (#396 spike, `artifacts/android/396-collection/spike/`).
- A card prefab carries its own `Canvas`, so a `RectMask2D` above it does not clip it: the Collection grid draws first and opaque header and footer bands over it hide the cards scrolled past its edges. The backend disables its `GraphicRaycaster`s and turns raycasts off through a `CanvasGroup`, or the card eats the cell's tap and the scroll drag. Its `AspectRatioFitter` resizes the root against the parent every layout pass, so the backend sizes the root itself and disables the fitter.
- The first `JsonGameDataManager.GetCardMap()` call of a game session holds the main thread 5–35 s on the emulator while the game deserializes every card (`android.collection.catalog_built map_ms`); the panel shows its loading text for a frame first. Text typed into the search field through `adb shell input text` reached the filter only on the soft keyboard's OK; a real keyboard was not tried.
- The Android back key reaches the game as Escape, and `PopupManager.HandleEscape` opens the escape menu over whatever BPP shows. `BackKeyPatches` prefixes it and `PopupManager.OnOpenSettings`; the touch root also reads `Keyboard.escapeKey` each frame, and `AndroidBackKey` stamps a consumed press with its frame so one press seen by both closes only one surface.
- Live Build's refresh awaits the catalog with `ConfigureAwait(false)` and resumes on a pool thread; `AndroidLiveBuildPanel` posts the result to `AndroidMainThread` before `LiveBuildPanelState.CompleteRefresh`, and `android.live_build.refresh_completed` logs `fetched_on_main_thread` and `completed_on_main_thread`. A rented card's own `Canvas` escapes the list's `RectMask2D` here too, so the pinned row and an opaque band under the list hide the cards scrolled past its edges.
- The game's rendered tooltip text carries its own `<line-height=1.6em>` and `<size>` tags for full-size text. Appended at reduced size, they kept full-height lines and the enchant list overflowed a phone tooltip; `AndroidEnchantPreview` strips the line heights and scales the sizes (`ItemEnchantPreviewText.ScaleInlineSizes`).
