# Card

Card art fills the face; with no art, the card name fills it. A tile never captions the name: it stays in the title and alt text. The border carries the tier (`tier-*`; Gold is `tier-gold`, not `accent`), as a 3px frame lit at the top and shaded at the foot, with a dark rim inside and out; Diamond and Legendary glow faintly in their tier. The frame shows only the tier; the enchantment shows as its material animating over the illustration, with a still frame under reduced motion. On a tile the tier and enchantment words stay for screen readers and the title only. A card or skill that opens something lifts and glows in its tier on hover and keyboard focus; a static one does not move. Skills are round tiles in a tier ring with no caption; without art they show the first letter of their name. Inline chips put the name, tier word and enchantment word beside a decorative thumbnail.
