# Card

Card art fills the face; with no art, the card name fills it. A tile never captions the name: it stays in the title and alt text. The border carries the tier (`tier-*`; Gold is `tier-gold`, not `accent`), as a 3px frame lit at the top and shaded at the foot, with a dark rim inside and out; Diamond and Legendary glow faintly in their tier. The frame shows only the tier. The enchantment shows as its material animating over the illustration at every card size, with a still frame under reduced motion, and as a gem in the top corner (see Enchantment). On a tile the tier and enchantment words stay for screen readers and the title only. A card or skill that opens something lifts above its neighbours and glows in its tier on hover and keyboard focus; a static one does not move. A tile given a preview shows it beside itself in the top layer while a mouse rests on it or keyboard focus reaches it: a read-only CardDetail listing only the card's own enchantment. Touch has no hover, so a tap opens the full detail instead. Skills are round tiles in a tier ring with no caption; without art they show the first letter of their name. Inline chips put the name, tier word and enchantment word beside a decorative thumbnail.
