# Card

Card art fills the face; with no art, the card name fills it. The border carries the tier (`tier-*`; Gold is `tier-gold`, not `accent`). An enchanted card adds an inner ring in the enchantment's colour. On a tile the tier word and a narrow tile's enchantment word stay for screen readers and the title only, so a board of small tiles holds no broken words; a board tile at least 4rem wide shows the enchantment word along its foot. Skills are round and keep their name below. Inline chips put the name, tier word and enchantment word beside a decorative thumbnail.
