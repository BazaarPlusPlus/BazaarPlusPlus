# Enchantment

An enchantment reads as a gem: a faceted diamond in the game's colour (`enchant-*-mark` where the text value is lifted, else `enchant-*`). Where its name is written, the gem leads the localized word in a pill tinted and ringed with that colour; the word takes the text-safe `enchant-*` value. Restorative and Mossy share a hue, as do Golden and Shielded; their words tell them apart. An unenchanted item has no label. A card tile shows the gem alone in its top corner, beside the material animating over its art (see Card), so an enchantment stays visible where no material renders.

Tiny applies only to Medium and Large items and reduces board width by one slot (Medium 2→1, Large 3→2); Small items never take Tiny.
